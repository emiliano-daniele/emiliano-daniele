## Hi, I'm Emiliano `</>`

Software architect based in Alicante, Spain. I design and build complete systems — web applications, APIs, integrations and cloud migrations — with twenty years of experience and the speed of a team of AI agents.

### How I work

1. **Specification.** Every project starts with a living specification that aligns what will be built and gives the agents precise instructions.
2. **Orchestration.** Specialized agents work in parallel — backend, tests, security review — while I keep control of the architecture.
3. **Review and delivery.** Nothing reaches production without critical review, and everything stays traceable back to the specification.

- Co-founder of three companies: Dadatickets and NextBand (2016), Infinite Context (2018).
